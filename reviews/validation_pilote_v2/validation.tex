\texttt{pilot\_production\_001} \hfill Graine 51
\rubrique{Énoncé d'origine}
A workshop produces tables and chairs. Let x1 be the number of tables produced and x2 the number of chairs produced. Both quantities may take any nonnegative real value, including fractional values. The profit per table is 16 EUR, and the profit per chair is 33 EUR. Producing each table requires 9 h of labor and 4 kg of wood. Producing each chair requires 3 h of labor and 9 kg of wood. At most 82 h of labor and 85 kg of wood are available. Determine the production quantities that maximize total profit.

\textit{Pour cette validation, formuler le programme linéaire.}
\rubrique{Formulation en programmation linéaire}
\[\begin{aligned}
\max_{x_1,x_2\in\mathbb{R}}\quad& 16x_1 +33x_2 \\
\text{s.c.}\quad & 9x_1 +3x_2 \le 82 \\
\quad & 4x_1 +9x_2 \le 85 \\
& x_1 \ge 0 \\
& x_2 \ge 0 \\
\end{aligned}\]
